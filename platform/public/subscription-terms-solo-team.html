Trial, billing and cancellation

The once-only app trial lasts 14 days from enrollment, requires no card and does not automatically convert to a paid subscription. Accepting service terms or creating a trial workspace starts no paid subscription. To subscribe, an authorised manager must deliberately accept the subscription terms and complete Stripe-hosted checkout. The checkout displays the billing dates before confirmation. If more than 48 trial hours remain, checkout preserves the original trial end; in its final 48 hours, wait until the trial ends before subscribing so free days are not lost.

A paid subscription renews monthly until cancelled through Manage subscription. Cancellation at period end retains access for the period paid. Failed, unpaid, paused or ended subscriptions do not allow routine editing. Viewing, exports and available access-revocation/recovery controls remain available for exit. Subscription status is verified on the server; returning from checkout alone grants no access. Contact CCS about billing disputes, duplicate or incorrect charges, or service failure. Mandatory legal remedies are unaffected.

Stripe handles card details; never enter card information in workspace records. End any running subscription before deleting the business. Minimal trial/billing mappings and acceptance records remain for administration and to prevent repeated free trials; workspace deletion does not erase Stripe’s records.

Visits currently have one assigned worker and recurring schedules are finite. Check suitability and capacity before importing records.

Support is by business email. No 24-hour support, uptime, response-time or recovery-time guarantee, or automatic independent backup service is included. Managers must keep independent protected backups and keep their passwords separately. Accounts must remain secure; do not share sign-in links, bypass permissions, introduce malicious content or access another business’s records. Report security concerns promptly. CCS may restrict access needed to contain misuse or a security incident, while coordinating lawful record return and exit.

Retention, return and exit

Current records remain while the workspace exists or until removed through the available controls. The app retains up to twenty prior database revisions and two hundred in-record activity entries; command/access metadata remains with the workspace. These are capacity limits, not timed retention rules. Archiving a customer is not erasure; contact CCS for assistance with individual erasure requests.

Managers can export current records and download a photo-inclusive encrypted backup before deletion. Exported files remain under their keeper’s control. Backups omit sign-in permissions and external billing data, and must be stored safely with passwords kept separately. Workspace deletion removes active records, memberships, invitations, database recovery history and stored photo objects; a failed object cleanup requires operator follow-up.

The customer may request record return or deletion by business email and agree an exit date. Any legally required retained data must be identified and limited to that purpose. Provider backup/security copies may follow separate deletion cycles rather than immediate erasure; CCS will request applicable deletion, put retained copies beyond ordinary use where possible and explain known limitations. No unverified fixed provider-retention period is promised. Customer-downloaded files cannot be remotely erased. No term excludes rights or liabilities that law does not permit to be excluded.
