Why will a booking not save?

Check that the customer is active, the date and time are valid, the visit finishes within the day, and the assigned worker has enough capacity. Overlapping work for the same worker is refused. One-off jobs have one visit; recurring batches contain up to twelve visits.

Use the selected visit's recurring controls to pause, resume, cancel or edit pending future visits. Paused visits reserve no capacity; resuming checks availability again. Earlier, completed, started and cancelled visits remain unchanged.

Can each customer have their own cleaning checklist?

Yes. In Customers, open a customer and enter one task per line in Customer checklist. You can have up to thirty tasks, each up to two hundred characters. This list replaces the standard four tasks for newly booked visits. Leave it blank to use the standard checklist.

Each booked visit keeps its own copy. Updating a customer does not silently change existing visits or their completed ticks. To refresh a scheduled or paused visit, open it, choose Edit, tick Use customer’s latest checklist for this visit, then save. Its tasks restart unchecked; other visits stay unchanged. Changing a visit’s customer automatically uses the new customer’s checklist. Started and completed visits cannot be refreshed.

Why can I not complete a visit?

Assign a worker and start the visit first, then finish every checklist item. Future visits cannot be started early. Notes are locked once a visit is completed or cancelled.

What if my connection drops?

A failed response can happen after a change has saved. Restore the connection and retry the same action, or explicitly reload the saved records and inspect them before continuing. Avoid submitting different versions of an unconfirmed change.

If another tab or device changed the workspace, the app loads the latest records and asks you to review before retrying. A visible error does not mean it is safe to overwrite someone else's changes.

Does adding a staff record create a login?

No. A manager creates an email-bound staff sign-in link separately. Links expire after seven days. The staff member needs authorised hosting access too; this test site remains owner-only. Share links privately and never share the owner's login.

Managers can revoke a staff member's access and related sign-in links through Team. Workers should see only their own assigned work.

How do I keep a complete backup?

Managers can use Settings & backups → Download encrypted backup. This includes records and staff photo files. Choose a password of at least twelve characters and keep it separately; it cannot be recovered for you. Save the .qvb file outside this service. Backups are manual, not automatically scheduled. Restoration is allowed only in an empty workspace and does not restore staff sign-in permissions. A records-only JSON export omits photo files.

Can Quenvira email an invoice automatically?

The server email system is installed but sending is disabled until a verified provider and owner-approved testing are complete. Managers can review Email delivery history in Invoices. Accepted means the provider accepted a request; delivered means the recipient server accepted it, not that the customer read it.

Supplier settings include a separate automatic issue-and-queue option, which also requires automatic drafts. Missing email configuration or an invalid recipient leaves a draft for manager review. Test emails are restricted to approved recipients. The fictional demo never sends emails.

How do invoices and payments work?

Complete a visit, then create a draft invoice. Add supplier details in Settings before issuing a private test invoice. Issued details are locked; an unpaid issued invoice can be fully credited. Use Print / save PDF to keep a copy. These are private test documents while invoicing acceptance checks continue.

Prepare email copy opens a message for review using the customer's saved email address. Send it yourself from your business email app; no attachment is added automatically. If the email app does not open, use Copy message text. Recording a manual payment does not collect money.

Find and update your records

Search Customers by name, address or email. Search Team by name or cleaning role; the pencil Edit button opens their hours, role and photo. In Jobs, use status buttons and search to find a visit. Clear filters restores the full day.

Saving feedback appears at the top. Refresh loads changes from another device. If you have unsaved edits, Quenvira asks before discarding them. A visit's progress bar shows how many checklist tasks are complete.

How do I protect my records?

Managers can download a JSON snapshot in Settings & backups. Keep it private: exports can contain customer information. Recovery history keeps up to twenty earlier revisions in the same database. It is not an independent disaster-recovery backup. Restore a revision only after checking which later edits it replaces.

Report a problem clearly

Use the fictional-data walkthrough, then share this short report through the existing private testing conversation:

  1. Device and browser version.
  2. Page and the steps you took.
  3. What you expected, and what actually happened.
  4. Exact error text, time and whether reloading changed it.
  5. Pass, Fail or Blocked for the relevant walkthrough step.

Do not include passwords, sign-in links, customer exports, access codes or personal customer details. Real-account sign-in, actual phone usability and independent hosted recovery still need final acceptance.

Supplier details, issued test invoices and credit notes

In Settings & backups, choose Edit supplier details. Use fictional legal name, address and contact details while this private release is being tested. For a limited company supply its registration number; VAT invoices require a VAT-registered supplier profile.

Create an invoice draft from a completed visit, review it and choose Issue invoice. Issuing locks a copy of the supplier, customer, service date, line and totals. Later customer or supplier edits will not change it. Print / save PDF opens your browser's print facility; choose Save as PDF where available. It does not automatically attach a PDF to an email.

An unpaid issued invoice can receive one full credit note for a genuine correction or agreed full reduction. This reduces its balance to zero and prevents recording payment against it. Partial credits and refunds of paid invoices are not supported. Recovery cannot restore a revision that would change an issued financial record.

Documents remain marked for private testing. Commercial accounting and release acceptance are still outstanding.

Start here: one step at a time

Your workspace now opens on Start here. Add your staff first (include yourself if you work alone), add a customer, book the first job, then assign a team member. The current step follows the records already saved, so reloading or returning on another device resumes your progress. Optional customer details and repeat-booking settings can be expanded when needed.

Once setup is complete, Start here offers the next actions. Owner report keeps the management overview separate. Existing records are preserved.

Staff: My day

A signed-in staff account opens on its own jobs for today, with the job count, remaining work, customer locations and upcoming assignments. View my calendar opens the schedule; choose a date to look further ahead. Open a visit for instructions, notes and the checklist. Staff access still requires a valid email-bound invitation and permission to visit this private app.

Staff profile photos

When adding a team member, choose Take photo to use a phone’s camera, or Choose photo to pick an existing picture. A preview appears before saving. The image is centre-cropped to a square and resized on the device; the original file and its location metadata are not uploaded.

In Team, use Add photo or Change photo to update an existing member. Remove photo returns their profile to initials. Removing the current picture hides it from the app; earlier pictures can remain in private recovery history. Photos appear with staff names on reports, schedules, job details and the staff profile. Account permissions also apply to photo requests.

The native camera chooser depends on the phone and browser. If a phone format cannot be opened, choose a JPEG or PNG. A staff member can be saved without a photo.
