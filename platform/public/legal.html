Privacy notice

Our role and your business’s role

Charles Buckton is responsible for information used to administer Quenvira accounts, respond to enquiries and protect the service. For customer and staff records entered by a business, that business decides why the information is needed and who may use it. The intended production arrangement is for CCS Creative Technologies to process those records on that business’s documented instructions, under a customer data-processing agreement.

Information used and purposes

  • Sign-in identity, account email, workspace membership and access invitations: to identify authorised users, connect them to their business and manage access.
  • Business-entered names, customer contact/service addresses, staff roles, daily capacity, job schedules, notes and checklists: to organise and deliver the business’s work.
  • Optional staff portraits: to help identify assigned team members. Quenvira does not perform face recognition. The upload is resized and re-encoded to a small JPEG; the original file is not uploaded by the app.
  • Supplier details, invoice/credit records and manually recorded payment status: to prepare and manage the business’s documents. Recording payment does not move money.
  • Command identifiers, revision records and activity information: to prevent conflicting updates, diagnose faults and investigate misuse. The hosting/sign-in provider also processes service and device information under its own terms.

Lawful basis

We use account and business-contact information where needed to provide the requested service or take requested steps towards it. Service security, account administration and support rely on legitimate interests, balanced against individuals’ rights. Legal obligations apply where required. A customer business must identify its own lawful basis, provide its staff and customers with appropriate information and give us lawful processing instructions. Uploading a photo is optional; a photo must not be uploaded without the appropriate authority and lawful basis.

Access and recipients

Managers can access their business records. Staff receive their own profile and assigned visits with necessary service information; the app omits invoice prices, customer email and other businesses’ records. Privileged service/hosting access may be used for authorised support, security and legal requirements. Staff cannot use manager backups, recovery, exports or business-deletion controls.

The published service uses OpenAI’s managed hosting and sign-in, with Cloudflare runtime/database/object-storage bindings. The published Sites data-processing agreement governs hosted personal data. The applicable provider subprocessor list and the exact hosting location must be recorded for production customer onboarding; we do not promise UK-only storage. Provider terms describe safeguards for international processing, including UK transfer provisions.

Relevant provider information: Sites terms, hosted-data processing agreement, subprocessor list and OpenAI privacy policy. Information supplied in conversations to build/edit the app is separate from published-site hosted data; avoid supplying real customer records in development prompts.

Device storage and communications

The workspace code does not store its records or authentication tokens in browser local storage and adds no advertising or optional analytics trackers. Managed sign-in/hosting may use necessary cookies and service logs. The separate fictional demonstration uses session storage scoped to the current browser tab. Refreshing retains progress; other tabs have independent edits. Use Reset to restore the examples. Browser session-restoration features may retain a previous tab’s progress. Downloaded exports remain on your device. Email copies can still be prepared for review in your own email app. The server email system stores a private invoice-message snapshot, recipient, sending attempts and delivery/audit status in the business database. Resend is connected for restricted testing with fictional invoices and approved test recipients. Live customer invoice sending remains disabled. Before customer sending is enabled, the processing schedule must record the sender, provider processing and retention arrangements. Provider acceptance is not proof of delivery; delivery status is recorded separately when a verified provider event arrives. Do not send a real invoice to an example.com test address.

Retention, backups and deletion

Current workspace records remain while the workspace exists or until removed through the available controls. The app retains up to twenty prior database revisions and up to two hundred entries in the in-record activity list. Command/security metadata is retained with the workspace. These are not fixed time-based deletion schedules, and archiving a customer is not erasure: managers should request help with an individual erasure request where record-level deletion is required.

Managers can download an encrypted business backup containing current records and staff-photo files. It excludes sign-in permissions, invitations, previous revisions and provider logs. Encryption is performed on the manager’s device; the password is not sent to the app server. We cannot recover a lost backup password. Keep the file separately from this service and keep the password separately from the file. Downloaded backups are managed by the person who keeps them; workspace deletion cannot erase copies already downloaded.

Backups are manual. No automatic backup schedule or guaranteed recovery time is currently offered. Restoration is restricted to an empty workspace; it restores business records/photos and keeps the destination business name. Staff access must be granted again. A records-only JSON export omits photo files and is not a complete backup.

Deleting a workspace removes active application records, memberships, invitations, in-database recovery history and its stored photo objects. If object cleanup fails, operator follow-up is required. Provider security, backup or legally retained copies are governed by provider terms; immediate erasure of every infrastructure copy is not promised. We will assist with any required provider deletion request.
