Proposed business subscription terms

Version 22 October 2026 — preparation and sandbox testing only. Paid subscriptions are not active. This draft is not an invitation to place a live order.

Service and price

Proposed UK business service: customer records, staff assignments, finite recurring visits, checklists, completion notes, invoice/credit records, manual exports and protected business backups. Proposed price: £19 total per business per month. CCS Creative Technologies is not registered for UK VAT (confirmed by the owner on 23 October 2026). No VAT is added to the £19 total monthly price. No online collection of your cleaning customers’ invoice payments, automatic invoice-email delivery, accounting integration, native app-store app, unlimited storage, medical or safeguarding records are included.

Trial, payment and cancellation

The proposed app trial lasts 14 days from workspace enrollment and requires no card. It does not convert automatically into a paid subscription. To subscribe, an authorised manager must accept final terms and complete Stripe-hosted checkout. If more than 48 hours of the trial remain, checkout preserves its original end date; during its last 48 hours the manager waits until the trial ends before subscribing, to avoid losing free days. A subscription renews monthly until cancelled through Manage subscription. Cancellation at period end retains access for the period paid. Failed, unpaid, paused or ended subscriptions do not permit routine editing; viewing, exports, access revocation and the available recovery controls remain accessible.

Stripe handles payment details. Never enter payment-card data into customer records. Subscription status is checked on the server; a checkout return page alone does not grant access. A business with a running subscription must end it before deleting its workspace. A minimal billing mapping and acceptance/receipt history remain for billing administration and to prevent repeated free trials; deletion of workspace records does not erase payment-provider records.

Operation and limits

Each account creates one initial business. A workspace record document is limited to 512 KiB; the encrypted business-backup format has a 20 MiB unencrypted limit and includes supported staff-photo files. Assignments currently use one worker per visit and recurring visits are finite. Support is by business email. No 24-hour support, uptime guarantee, automatic independent backup service or recovery-time guarantee is offered. Managers must save protected backups separately and keep their passwords safely.

Data processing, rights and exit

The customer controls its customer/staff records and must have authority and a lawful basis to enter them. CCS processes those records on documented instructions, restricts access to authorised people, protects confidentiality, assists with applicable rights/security obligations, reports personal-data breaches to the customer without undue delay and supports return/deletion at service end. The customer retains rights in its records; CCS retains applicable software and documentation rights, subject to third-party licences.

Hosting/sign-in is through OpenAI managed Sites with Cloudflare storage/runtime bindings. Final onboarding must identify the actual provider/subprocessor chain, transfer safeguards and applicable locations; no UK-only residency is promised. The final customer DPA and processing schedule must record customer identity, authorised instructions, provider authorisation, retention/deletion arrangements and exit dates. Those particulars are not yet complete, so this draft is insufficient for live customer onboarding.

Managers can export current records and download a photo-inclusive encrypted backup. Backup files exclude sign-in memberships and external subscription/billing information. Customer-directed exit/deletion timing and legally required retention must be recorded in the final order. Provider backups and copies kept by customers follow their respective deletion arrangements. See the current privacy and pilot notice.
