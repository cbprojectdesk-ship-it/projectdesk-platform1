## DPA clauses prepared for incorporation into an agreed order

1. **Instructions:** process customer personal data only for the documented instructions and processing schedule, including instructions for transfers; flag an instruction believed to be unlawful and notify the controller of a binding disclosure requirement where legally permitted.
2. **Confidentiality:** restrict access to authorised people under duties of confidentiality, for the stated service purpose.
3. **Security:** maintain measures proportionate to the documented risk and current service; disclose material limitations, including the manual nature of independent backups. Do not advertise an uptime or recovery guarantee not supported by an agreed operating routine.
4. **Subprocessors:** identify and obtain the customer's required authorisation for the actual hosting/provider chain; provide the agreed change-notice route and apply equivalent processing obligations. Record unresolved objections before continuing affected processing.
5. **Assistance:** reasonably assist the controller with rights requests, security assessments, DPIAs and regulator consultation where applicable, considering the processing and information available.
6. **Incident notification:** notify the controller without undue delay after awareness of a personal-data breach, share available facts, affected scope, mitigation/contact and ongoing updates; preserve evidence. Do not substitute a fixed 72-hour processor deadline for the applicable controller duties.
7. **Return/deletion:** at service end, follow the controller's choice and agreed dates for return or deletion, except legally required retention; identify copies put beyond use and the provider/backup deletion cycle rather than promising immediate erasure of all copies.
8. **Compliance information:** provide proportionate information and allow the applicable agreed audit/inspection rights with confidentiality safeguards. Record any provider restrictions or costs rather than asserting unrestricted access to provider infrastructure.

Customer identity, instructions, provider details and the parties' agreement must be completed before this becomes an operational DPA. This is a prepared review text; no customer or supplier agreement has been signed or accepted by the assistant.
