## Billing review operation

The signed handler now recognises charge.refunded, charge.dispute.created and radar.early_fraud_warning.created. It retrieves Charge → PaymentIntent → InvoicePayments → Invoice.parent.subscription_details.subscription → Subscription, verifies mode/customer consistency, then maps the provider customer to the stored business. Metadata cannot select a workspace. Provider failures return an error for Stripe retry. Unlinked one-off payments are ignored. The handler stores only the existing event ID/business/type/receipt fields, not payment details or the full event. Duplicate receipts remain idempotent. Risk receipts are excluded from the routine 30-day lifecycle receipt pruning so an unreviewed case cannot silently disappear. The manager billing panel discloses that a review was recorded; staff cannot view it. This is a historical review indicator, not an unresolved-case count or proof of fraud.

At activation, explicitly subscribe the processing webhook to these three events and grant the restricted key Charges read, Invoice Payments/Invoices read, and Subscriptions read in addition to existing Checkout/Customer/Portal permissions. Verify exact provider permission labels and delivery in sandbox first. Keep the verification-only live endpoint separate; it acknowledges signatures without processing billing. A review requires CCS to inspect the Stripe case, reconcile any partial/full refund, check the dispute response deadline and decide the appropriate customer communication or access action. This implementation makes no automatic refund, dispute submission, fraud finding or access suspension. Record the operator outcome in the case and do not treat the historical in-app indicator as open/closed case status.

Real direct-debit collection, mandate acceptance, settlement, failure and dispute outcomes remain unverified. Dynamic payment methods are not hardcoded. Do not advertise direct debit as activated or fee-free. The invoice settlement guard is method-independent and keeps paid access closed while settlement is pending. Preserve the recorded UK non-VAT-registered facts; automatic tax stays off pending any actual registration change.
